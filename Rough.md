## How many multiples of n from $x$ to $y$,inclusive? ($x$ < $y$)

$$
\frac{a - b}{n} + 1
$$

where

$a$ is the smallest multiple of n between x and y.

$b$ is the largest multiple of n between x and y.

## No. of positive factors

If $n=a^x \times b^y \times c^z$

Then no.of positive factors of $n$ = $(x+1) \times (y+1) \times (z+1)$

Eg: 

$$
360=2^{3}\times 3^{2}\times 5^{1}
$$

No. of positive factors = $(3+1)\times (2+1)\times (1+1) = 4\times 3\times 2 = 24$
